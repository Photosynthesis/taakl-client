Taakl — design concepts

Four directions for the visual redesign. Each shows the Plan and Do views (switchable via the nav) with identical sample data, plus a live running session.
Concept 1

Analog Ledger

The review-screen mockup's aesthetic extended app-wide: warm paper ground, Fraunces serif headings, IBM Plex mono numerals, hairline rules, grain. A beautifully kept field notebook; the live session reads as red-ink annotation.
Concept 2

Quiet Instrument

The current design distilled further: near-monochrome warm white, structure from whitespace rather than boxes, one refined green accent, warm signal color reserved for the live timer. Instrument Sans + light tabular mono figures.
Concept 3

Night Console

A dark instrument panel for long sessions: warm charcoal, mono-forward chrome, amber interactions, phosphor green strictly for the running session, tmux-style status bar with keybinding hints. Professional equipment, low glare.
Concept 4

Soft Studio

The structural departure: a left sidebar replaces the top nav, and every top-level project owns a muted hue used across dots, tags, and chips. Sage-mist ground, white rounded cards, Bricolage Grotesque headings. Calm, tactile, tidy.
In every concept: Plan/Do in the nav actually switch views, the elapsed timer ticks, and checkboxes toggle done styling. Assistant and Review are inert. Serve statically (e.g. php -S localhost:8000 in taakl-client/) and open /mockups/design-concepts.html, or open the files directly.
